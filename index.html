Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Webora Web Services handles information when you contact or use our WhatsApp chatbot.

Effective date: November 8, 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect information that is needed to respond to you and operate the chatbot. Depending on how you use it, this may include:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and relevant account or message identifiers.
  • The messages and prompts you send, along with replies and conversation history needed to maintain context and improve continuity.
  • Files, images, voice notes, or other content you choose to send. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.
  • Basic interaction and technical details, such as message timestamps, delivery status, and limited diagnostic logs.

WhatsApp and its providers may also collect information under their own privacy terms when you use their platform.

2. How we use your information

We use information to provide chatbot responses and requested services, remember relevant conversation context, maintain and secure the service, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support requests. We may also use limited, appropriately protected information to assess and improve the chatbot’s quality.

3. AI and conversation history

The chatbot uses AI to interpret messages and generate responses. Your message and relevant conversation context may be processed by an AI service provider to produce a reply. AI-generated responses can be inaccurate, so please verify important information.

We may keep conversation history and operational logs for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your conversation information; some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

4. When information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share the minimum information necessary with:

  • WhatsApp / Meta: to send and receive messages through WhatsApp.
  • AI and infrastructure providers: to process chatbot requests, host or operate the service, and keep it secure.
  • Authorities or other parties: when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect people, rights, and the security of the service.

Service providers are expected to use information only to provide services to us and to protect it appropriately. Their own privacy terms may also apply.

5. Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No messaging service or online storage can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is legally required.

6. Your choices and requests

You can stop using the chatbot at any time. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your WhatsApp number, subject to applicable law and necessary retention. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

7. Children’s privacy

This chatbot is not designed for children who are not permitted to use WhatsApp or the service under applicable law. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the chatbot.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the chatbot or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated effective date.
